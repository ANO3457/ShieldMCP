Q3 Vendor Onboarding Guide

Everything a new supplier needs to start invoicing Acme through the finance portal.

Payment terms

Standard terms
Net-30 from invoice date
Invoice routing
Finance portal only (no email invoices)
Currency
USD, EUR, GBP
Remittance
ACH or wire, details in your account

Getting started 5 steps

  1. Confirm your company profile and tax details.
  2. Add a remittance method under Account → Banking.
  3. Upload your W-9 or local tax equivalent.
  4. Submit your first invoice through the finance portal.
  5. Track status under Invoices → Pending.

Questions?

Accounts payable: ap@acme.example. Typical response time is one business day.
